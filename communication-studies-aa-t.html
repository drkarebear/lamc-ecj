Associate Degree for Transfer

Communication Studies AA-T

Build strong speaking, listening, argumentation, and interpersonal communication skills while preparing to transfer as a Communication Studies major.

This Program Might Be a Good Fit If You…

  • Enjoy speaking, presenting, discussing ideas, or working with people
  • Want to become a stronger listener, collaborator, and communicator
  • Are interested in media, culture, relationships, persuasion, or public messages
  • Are considering transferring as a Communication Studies major or into a related field

AA-T stands for Associate in Arts for Transfer. If you meet CSU transfer eligibility requirements and earn the degree, you are guaranteed admission to a CSU campus and receive priority admission consideration. The guarantee does not apply to a particular CSU campus or major.

What You'll Learn to Do

  • Communicate effectively with different audiences
  • Analyze how messages influence people and communities
  • Build stronger arguments and evaluate evidence
  • Adapt your communication for interpersonal, professional, cultural, group, and digital settings

Where Communication Skills Can Lead

Communication skills support careers in business, marketing, public relations, media, law, education, journalism, human resources, nonprofit work, public service, and many other people-centered fields.

What Classes Will I Take?

The Communication Studies AA-T requires 18 units in the major. A unit is the credit value assigned to a college course. Some classes may count toward both the Communication Studies major and your general education requirements—often called double-counting. Cal-GETC is the California General Education Transfer Curriculum used for transfer to CSU and UC. A counselor can help you confirm which courses apply to your plan.

1 Take These 2 Required Communication Courses

Official degree language: Required Core

Complete both courses:

  • COMM C1000 – Public Speaking (3 units)
  • COMM 121 – Interpersonal Communication (3 units)

2 Choose 3 Communication Courses

Official degree language: List A

Select three courses:

  • COMM 104 – Argumentation and Debate (3 units)
  • COMM 151 – Small Group Communication (3 units)
  • COMM 122 – Intercultural Communication (3 units)
  • COMM 100 – Introduction to Communication Studies (3 units)
  • COMM 190 – Communication and New Media (3 units) Coming Spring 2027 Check with Counseling before including this course in your current plan.
  • COMM 130 – Introduction to Oral Interpretation of Literature (3 units)
  • COMM 102 – Oral Communication II (3 units)
  • COMM 109 – Social Media Strategies (3 units) Coming Spring 2027 Check with Counseling before including this course in your current plan.

3 Choose 1 More Course That Fits Your Goals

Official degree language: List B

Choose one course below, or choose another unused course from the List A options above. Pick an option that fits your interests and transfer goals.

  • ANTHRO 102 – Human Ways of Life: Cultural Anthropology (3 units)
  • PSYC C1000 – Introduction to Psychology (3 units)
  • SOC 001 – Introduction to Sociology (3 units)
  • ENGLISH 102 – College Reading and Composition II (3 units)
  • ENGL C1001 – Critical Thinking and Writing (3 units)
  • JOURNAL 101 – Collecting and Writing News (3 units)
