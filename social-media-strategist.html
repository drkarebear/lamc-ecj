Certificate of Achievement

Social Media Strategist Certificate

Turn creativity into strategy with practical skills in content, audiences, marketing, and digital communication.

This Certificate Might Be a Good Fit If You…

  • enjoy creating social media content and thinking about why some posts connect with audiences
  • are interested in marketing, public relations, writing, media, or digital storytelling
  • want practical skills for managing social media for an organization, client, business, or your own projects
  • like combining creativity with strategy, audience awareness, and technology

What You'll Learn to Do

  • Plan social media content and campaigns around goals, audiences, and platforms
  • Write and adapt content for different digital audiences and communities
  • Create and coordinate multimedia content across social platforms
  • Use ideas from marketing, public relations, analytics, and SEO to evaluate and improve strategy

Where Social Media Strategy Skills Can Lead

These skills can support entry-level and early-career work in social media coordination and strategy, content creation, digital marketing, public relations, brand communication, and community management. They can also strengthen freelance, entrepreneurial, and transfer pathways in communication, marketing, media, journalism, and related fields.

What Classes Will I Take?

To earn the Social Media Strategist Certificate of Achievement, complete all 8 required 3-unit courses, for 24 units total. There are no electives in this certificate.

New certificate: some LAMC webpages still show earlier requirements. The catalog addendum and approved program record list 8 required courses / 24 units. Confirm your current plan with Counseling before enrolling.
