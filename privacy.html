Privacy Statement

How to protect your privacy while using this faculty-maintained ECJ website and its interactive guides.

Protect your privacy: Do not enter passwords, Social Security numbers, student ID numbers, medical records, financial account information, or other sensitive personal information into an interactive guide on this site.

About This Website

This website is maintained by faculty in the Los Angeles Mission College English, Communication Studies & Journalism Department as a resource for students and the community. It is not an official Los Angeles Mission College or Los Angeles Community College District website.

The website itself does not provide user accounts, registration forms, advertising, or first-party analytics, and it does not ask visitors to submit student records through this site.

ECJ Class Finder

The ECJ Class Finder reads a static copy of public LACCD class-schedule information stored with this website. The class-finder page does not ask for a student login, student ID, or other enrollment credentials. When you choose a link to check a section in the official Student Information System, you leave this website and the destination site follows its own privacy practices.

Website Hosting

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other web-hosting providers, GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited. See GitHub’s information about GitHub Pages for details.

Optional PlayLab Interactive Guides

The Program Finder and Interactive Support Guide use PlayLab, an external AI service. These tools are optional. They are set to load only after you choose the load button, so you can read this site and use its ordinary links without loading a PlayLab guide.

When you choose to load a PlayLab guide, your browser connects to PlayLab. PlayLab may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and usage information, and it may use cookies or analytics technologies according to its own practices. Anything you type into a PlayLab guide is sent to PlayLab and may be stored or made available according to the service’s settings and privacy practices.

Keep information general. Do not enter your name, student ID number, password, Social Security number, contact information, medical or disability information, financial account information, immigration information, or other sensitive personal details. For the Support Guide, a broad request such as “tutoring,” “food assistance,” “counseling,” “financial aid,” or “help choosing classes” is enough.

For more information, review PlayLab’s Privacy Policy.

External Links

This site also links to official LAMC and LACCD pages and to other outside resources. When you follow an external link, the destination site handles the information associated with that visit under its own privacy practices.

Official College Services

For official college records, services, policies, or matters involving private student information, use official Los Angeles Mission College systems and offices rather than an interactive guide on this site.
