Certificate of Achievement

Journalism Certificate

Build practical skills in reporting, writing, editing, public relations, social media, and digital storytelling.

This Certificate Might Be a Good Fit If You…

  • like asking questions, finding out what happened, and explaining it clearly
  • enjoy writing, interviewing, photography, video, audio, or social media
  • want to learn how to evaluate sources, evidence, and bias
  • are interested in journalism, public relations, digital media, or content creation

This certificate combines journalism, public relations, social media, visual communication, and digital storytelling so you can practice communicating accurately across several media formats.

What You'll Learn to Do

  • Research, report, write, and edit accurate stories
  • Evaluate sources, evidence, and potential bias
  • Create multimedia stories for digital audiences
  • Use social media, visual media, and public relations strategies professionally

Where Journalism Skills Can Lead

These skills can support work or further study in journalism, writing, digital media, public relations, social media, and content creation. They also build transferable skills in research, interviewing, source evaluation, clear writing, and audience awareness.

What Classes Will I Take?

To earn the Journalism Certificate of Achievement, take 6 required 3-unit courses and choose 1 elective, for 21 units total. You do not need to choose among the required courses—they are all part of the certificate. Your one elective lets you add photography, creative nonfiction, or podcasting.

A unit is a measure of college credit. The term suggestions below are planning guides, not guarantees that a course will be offered in that term.
