Interactive Program Finder

Which Program Might Fit You?

Not sure which ECJ path fits your interests or goals? Answer a few questions and use the suggestions as a starting point for exploring English, Communication Studies, Creative Writing, Journalism, and Social Media Strategist programs.

Start With What Sounds Like You

You do not need to have a major picked out. Start with what you enjoy, what you want to learn, or what you hope to do next.

“I like writing and storytelling, but I’m not sure what I want to do with it.”

“I want a program that could help me transfer to a four-year college.”

“I’m interested in media, journalism, or social media.”

Use this as a starting point: The finder can help you explore possibilities, but it does not replace academic advising. A counselor can help you confirm requirements and build an education plan.

Start the Interactive Program Finder

Answer the questions in your own words. You can explore more than one possibility and then open the program pages to compare your options.

This optional interactive guide is powered by PlayLab, an external AI service. Its suggestions are for exploration and are not official academic advising. Do not include private student information or other sensitive personal details.

Interactive Program Finder

Load the Optional PlayLab Program Finder?

The finder has not loaded yet. If you choose Load Program Finder, your browser will connect to PlayLab, which may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and usage information. Anything you type into the finder will also be sent to PlayLab.

You can browse every degree and certificate on this website without loading PlayLab. Do not enter your name, student ID, contact information, or other sensitive personal information. Read the full privacy statement.

If the embedded finder does not load, you can open the Program Finder directly in PlayLab or return to the full program list. Open the Program Finder directly in PlayLab or Browse all ECJ programs.
