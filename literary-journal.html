LAMC’s Student Literary Journal

La Misión Review

La Misión Review celebrates the fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, drama, photography, and visual art of Los Angeles Mission College students and alumni.

Submit Creative Work Visit the Official Journal Page

About the Journal

Published by the English Department, the magazine creates a home for student expression and a literary arts community in Sylmar, the San Fernando Valley, and beyond.

See how the journal works each year · Read recent issues · Submit creative work

The Annual Cycle

How La Misión Review Works Each Year

The journal follows the same simple rhythm each academic year: submissions in the fall, production in the spring.

Every Fall

September 1–December 31: Submit Your Work

Current LAMC students and alumni may submit fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, drama, photography, and visual art during the fall submission period.

See Submission Details

Every Spring

Students Put the Next Issue Together

Students in English 32: Literary Magazine Editing work together during the spring semester to help edit and assemble the next issue of La Misión Review.

Submit Your Work: September 1–December 31

La Misión Review accepts submissions every fall from September 1 through December 31. Use the official submission form for current requirements and instructions.

Before You Submit

  • You must be a current LAMC student or an LAMC alumnus or alumna.
  • Accepted work includes fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, drama, photography, and visual art.
  • Use the official submission form for current instructions and requirements.
  • Questions may be sent privately to lamreview@lamission.edu.

Help Create the Journal in Spring

Creative Contributors

Submit fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, drama, photography, or visual art. Current students and alumni of LAMC are eligible.

Take English 32 in Spring

English 32: Literary Magazine Editing gives students hands-on experience helping to edit and assemble the journal. The course also counts toward the Creative Writing Certificate.
