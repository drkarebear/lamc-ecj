Associate Degree for Transfer

English AA-T

Build strong writing, literary analysis, and critical-thinking skills while preparing to transfer as an English major.

This Program Might Be a Good Fit If You…

  • Enjoy reading, writing, language, or storytelling
  • Like discussing ideas and exploring different interpretations
  • Want to strengthen writing, research, and critical-thinking skills
  • Are considering transferring as an English major or into a related field

AA-T stands for Associate in Arts for Transfer. If you meet CSU transfer eligibility requirements and earn the degree, you are guaranteed admission to a CSU campus and receive priority admission consideration. The guarantee does not apply to a particular CSU campus or major.

What You'll Learn to Do

  • Analyze literature, language, culture, and media
  • Write clearly and persuasively for different audiences
  • Develop evidence-based interpretations and arguments
  • Apply strong reading, writing, and research skills across disciplines

Where English Skills Can Lead

English skills support careers in education, law, publishing, media, writing, editing, communications, marketing, nonprofit work, public service, and creative industries.

What Classes Will I Take?

The English AA-T requires 18–21 units in the major. A unit is the credit value assigned to a college course. Some classes may count toward both the English major and your general education requirements— often called double-counting. Cal-GETC is the California General Education Transfer Curriculum used for transfer to CSU and UC. A counselor can help you confirm which courses apply to your plan.

Important: These are the major requirements, not your entire associate degree. You will also complete general education and other college graduation requirements. A counselor can help you see how the pieces fit together.

1 Start With 1 College Composition Course

Official degree language: prerequisite requirement

Complete one of the following:

  • ENGL C1000 – Academic Reading and Writing (3 units)
  • ENGL C1000E – Academic Reading and Writing (3 units)
  • ENGLISH 101Z – College Reading and Composition I Plus 3-Hour Lab (4 units)
  • E.S.L. 110 – College Composition for Non-Native Speakers (4 units)

2 Take These 2 Required Courses

Official degree language: Required Core

Complete both courses:

  • ENGL C1001 – Critical Thinking and Writing (3 units)
  • ENGLISH 102 – College Reading and Composition II (3 units)

3 Choose 2 Literature Courses

Official degree language: List A
