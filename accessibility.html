Accessibility

How this faculty-maintained website approaches accessible design, testing, and third-party services.

Accessibility target: This faculty-maintained website aims to meet or exceed WCAG 2.2 Level AA where the project itself controls the experience.

Our Accessibility Approach

I aim to make this faculty-maintained ECJ website usable by as many people as possible, using WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the design target where this site controls the experience. The site is built with semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, large interaction targets, and reduced-motion support.

Embedded and External Services

Some pages link to or embed services maintained by other providers, including Los Angeles Mission College websites and PlayLab interactive guides. Those services may have different accessibility features or barriers and are governed by their own accessibility practices.

The PlayLab guides are optional and do not load until a visitor chooses the load button. Students can use the program pages, Student Resources page, official LAMC Student Services directory, and LAMC Counseling without loading an embedded guide. See the Privacy Statement for information about third-party data handling.

Testing and Limitations

WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the project target. Current review includes source-level checks for headings, landmarks, accessible names, form labels, focus support, internal links, responsive structure, reduced motion, and HTML/CSS/JavaScript validity, plus rendered checks for text contrast, interaction-target sizing, and horizontal overflow at desktop and mobile widths. These checks do not certify accessibility or legal compliance. Hands-on keyboard, zoom/reflow, screen-reader, and third-party-content testing should continue as the site changes.

Report an Accessibility Barrier

If something on this ECJ website is difficult to access or use, please email Karen Crozer at crozerkj@lamission.edu, the faculty member who maintains this site. I want to fix barriers when I can.

Email Karen Crozer About an Accessibility Issue

If possible, include the page address, what you were trying to do, and a short description of what happened. You do not need to share information about a disability, diagnosis, or medical condition.

If you prefer to use an official college contact route, you can also visit the official ECJ Department page on the LAMC website.
