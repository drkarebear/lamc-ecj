Accessibility

How this faculty-maintained website approaches accessible design, testing, and third-party services.

Accessibility target: This faculty-maintained website aims to meet or exceed WCAG 2.2 Level AA where the project itself controls the experience.

Accessibility

I aim to make this faculty-maintained ECJ Degrees & Certificates website usable by as many people as possible and to meet or exceed WCAG 2.2 Level AA where this site controls the experience. The site uses semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, high-contrast text and controls, responsive reflow, large interaction targets, and reduced-motion support.

Embedded and External Services

Some pages link to or embed services maintained by other providers, including Los Angeles Mission College websites and PlayLab interactive guides. Those services may have different accessibility features or barriers and are governed by their own accessibility practices.

The PlayLab guides are optional and do not load until a visitor chooses the load button. Students can use the program pages, Student Resources page, official LAMC Student Services directory, and LAMC Counseling without loading an embedded guide. See the Privacy Statement for information about third-party data handling.

Testing and Limitations

I use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the project target and test for common WAVE errors, contrast errors, keyboard barriers, heading and landmark problems, reflow problems, accessible-name issues, and reduced-motion support. Automated tools cannot certify legal compliance, so human review and assistive-technology testing remain part of the accessibility process.

Report an Accessibility Barrier

If you encounter an accessibility barrier in this faculty-maintained website, please email me about the accessibility issue and, if possible, include the page and a short description of what was difficult to use.
