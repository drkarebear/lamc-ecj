Need Help? Start Here.

If school, money, technology, health, family, or something else is making college harder, you do not have to figure it out alone. Start with what you need today.

Need Urgent or Emergency Help?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

On or near the LAMC campus: You can also contact the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department at LAMC at (818) 364-7843. Campus safety coverage is available 24 hours a day.

Need immediate emotional support? Call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Free, confidential support is available 24/7. You do not have to be suicidal to contact 988.

Call, Text, or Chat With the 988 Lifeline →

Optional Interactive Support Guide

Choose the Kind of Help You Need

Use a broad category rather than describing your personal story. The optional guide can suggest LAMC resources that may fit and give you a place to start.

“Tutoring or academic support.” “Food assistance or basic needs.” “Help choosing classes.”

You do not need to know the name of an office or program.

Protect your privacy: The guide uses PlayLab, an external AI service. Anything you type into it is sent to PlayLab. Keep requests general and do not enter your name, student ID, contact information, medical or disability information, financial information, immigration information, passwords, or other sensitive personal details. Read the privacy statement.
