Certificate of Achievement

Creative Writing Certificate

Turn ideas into finished work while exploring creative genres, editing, publishing, journalism, multimedia, and digital writing.

This Certificate Might Be a Good Fit If You…

  • Enjoy writing stories, memoir, scripts, essays, or other creative work
  • Want regular practice, feedback, and deadlines to help you finish your writing
  • Are interested in publishing, editing, journalism, media, or content creation
  • Want to build a portfolio while exploring different kinds of writing

The Creative Writing Certificate of Achievement is a focused 18-unit certificate. You can shape your six-course path around the genres, forms, and creative experiences that interest you most.

What You'll Learn to Do

  • Develop original creative work in different genres and forms
  • Revise and edit writing with greater purpose and control
  • Experiment with voice, audience, storytelling, and structure
  • Build a portfolio of creative and professional writing

Where Creative Writing Skills Can Lead

Creative writing builds storytelling, revision, audience awareness, editing, and project-completion skills. Those skills can support work in publishing, entertainment, journalism, editing, marketing, communications, content creation, and media , as well as independent creative projects and further study.

What Classes Will I Take?

Choose 6 approved 3-unit courses (18 units total) from the options below. You do not need to take every course in the table. Use the “Typically Offered” column as a planning guide; schedules can change, so check the current LAMC schedule and talk with Counseling before building your semester plan.

Create & Publish at LAMC

Put Your Writing and Editing Skills Into Practice with La Misión Review

La Misión Review, launched in 2025, is the Los Angeles Mission College English Department's first student literary journal. It showcases creative work by LAMC students and alumni.

Students can submit creative work for publication or take ENGLISH 32: College Literary Magazine Editing in the spring to help review submissions, edit pieces, organize the issue, and prepare the journal for publication.

Make it count: ENGLISH 32 counts toward the Creative Writing Certificate and can also be used as a List C option for the English AA-T.
