Interactive Support Guide

Find the Right Place to Start

Choose the kind of help you need in broad terms. The guide can help you find Los Angeles Mission College resources that may fit—you do not need to know which office to ask.

Start With the Kind of Help You Need

You do not need college vocabulary or the name of an office. A broad category such as tutoring, food assistance, counseling, financial aid, or class planning is enough.

“Tutoring or academic support.”

“Food assistance or help with basic needs.”

“Help choosing classes for next semester.”

Protect your privacy: This optional guide is provided through PlayLab, an external AI service. Anything you type into the guide is sent to PlayLab and may be stored according to its privacy practices. Use a broad category of need rather than your personal story. Do not include your name, student ID, contact information, password, Social Security number, medical or disability information, financial account information, immigration information, or other sensitive personal details. Read the privacy statement.
Need immediate or crisis help?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. On or near campus, you can also call the LAMC Sheriff at (818) 364-7843.

For immediate emotional or crisis support, call or text 988, or chat with the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. You do not have to be suicidal to use 988.

Start the Interactive Support Guide

Enter only a broad category of help, then use the suggestions as a starting point for finding real LAMC people and services.

This optional guide is powered by PlayLab, an external AI service. It can suggest places to start, but it is not a counselor, official college system, or emergency service.

Want a Different Way to Get Help?

You never have to use the interactive guide. Browse resources yourself, ask the Eagles Help Desk where to start, or open LAMC’s official Student Services directory.
