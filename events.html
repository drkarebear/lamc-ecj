Have something happening?

If English faculty, writers, readers, or students across the district might reasonably be interested, send it our way. Adjunct and full-time faculty are equally welcome to submit. You do not need to run an event through a DDC representative first.

Submissions are reviewed before posting. Event information you submit is intended for public display. Your verification email stays in the private moderation Sheet and is not included in the public event feed.
